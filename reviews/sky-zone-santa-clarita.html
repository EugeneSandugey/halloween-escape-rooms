★☆☆☆☆ · Google · 2026-10-11
I’ve noticed an ongoing issue at Sky Zone in Santa Clarita involving older kids, roughly ages 10–15, being disrespectful and inconsiderate toward much younger children. At the foam pit, groups of older kids frequently take over the round inflatable ball platforms, leaving little room or opportunity for younger children to participate. What’s especially concerning is that I’ve witnessed them physically pushing smaller children, including my son, off the platforms as soon as they land their jumps nearby. I’ve also witnessed similar behavior on the balance beam located across from those platforms in the foam pit. I understand that kids of different ages come here to have fun, but younger children should be able to enjoy the equipment without being pushed around or intimidated by older kids. This isn’t just about sharing or taking turns; it’s a safety concern. What disappoints me most is the lack of intervention from staff when these situations occur. Rather than standing by and watching these incidents unfold, employees should take the initiative to step in, address the behavior, and ensure that younger children can safely enjoy the attractions too. It shouldn’t take a parent having to intervene for something to be done.

I absolutely teach my kids to walk away and get an adult whenever possible, but I also teach them that they are allowed to protect themselves if someone is physically attacking them and they cannot safely get away. I’m not leaving this review because my kids were asked to leave. I’m leaving it because situations involving children deserve to be handled carefully and fairly. Staff should intervene, separate everyone, listen to both sides, and, when possible, speak with witnesses before deciding who started something. Hopefully management can use situations like this as an opportunity to improve how these incidents are handled in the future.

★★☆☆☆ · Google · 2025-12-13
We were very excited when we heard that a Sky Zone was opening in our neighborhood. Before the grand opening, we purchased a membership through the website, and today was already our third visit. Our kids always have a great time here, the staff is friendly, and the facility is clean and well maintained. That said, there are a few downsides. The location is smaller compared to other Sky Zone locations, and on weekends there is often a wait outside. While the space is open to all age groups, there tends to be a high number of middle and high school students. This can make it somewhat challenging for younger kids to enjoy all areas comfortably. For example, the basketball court is often dominated by older kids, making it difficult for younger children to participate. The play can also get quite aggressive at times, which makes some younger kids feel intimidated. As you can see in the photos, most younger children tend to stay around the trampoline areas, while older kids gather around the basketball court and other sections. It would be great if there were time limits or scheduled rotations for shared areas so that children of all ages can enjoy the space more safely and fairly.
