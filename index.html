Red flags from the worst reviews
  • For 5–6 people skip The Elevator Shaft: it's cramped (they say 3–4 max) and the most-complained-about room — book The Basement or The Courtyard (The Study gets complaints too)
  • No hints on request: only the actor decides when to help, and games are 45 minutes (some groups never escape)
  • Puzzles or sensors that broke, with no time given back; very dark rooms
  • Customer service is the #1 complaint on Google and Yelp: rude or snippy staff (including toward a 15-year-old's birthday group, where they asked for copies of the parents' IDs because it's a horror room), long waits at check-in, slow refunds
  • Recent: a guest's arm injury brushed off by staff (Oct 2026); in 2024 an actor grabbed a guest by the neck
  • Weekends need at least 4 tickets (a group of 3 was turned away)
  • Strict no-touching rules for the actors; waivers must be done before you arrive

Anyone under 18 needs someone 18+ in the room; an 18-year-old friend counts.

Which room

Book The Basement (the original room) or The Courtyard.

  • The Elevator Shaft: cramped (they say 3–4 max), wet and dirty, very loud so you can't hear clues, claustrophobic; one 2024 group got bleach fumes that burned their eyes; hints stop at the last puzzle
