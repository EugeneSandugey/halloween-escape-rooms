Good to know

  • Ages: The Basement needs one person 18+ in the room (who also buys a ticket). Hypnotic's minimum age is 14. Greatest Escape needs an adult for anyone under 14.
  • Waivers: everyone signs online from the confirmation email. Do it before you leave; The Basement cuts game time if waivers hold you up at the door.
  • No refunds at The Basement or Enchanted. The Basement needs 48 hours' notice to reschedule. Book once the head count is final.
  • Arrive 10–15 minutes early.
  • Only The Basement's calendar was checked for open times. For the others, open "Book" to see what's left; Halloween weekend fills up.

How this was checked

Google Maps search for escape rooms from Lancaster to the San Fernando Valley. Searched every city in the San Fernando Valley plus Lancaster–Santa Clarita. For the 14 serious options, the 80 worst and 80 newest Google reviews each (every 1–3★ review they have), plus all of Yelp's shown reviews for The Basement (368), Arcane (210) and Architect (420), and a scan of the 4–5★ reviews for deal-breaker words (injury, touching, refunds, refusals, dirty, broken), with every 1–3 star review read. Rating-inflation check: how fast new reviews come in, how many name a staff member, and Google vs Yelp. Prices from each venue's site or booking page and Groupon's live deals.
